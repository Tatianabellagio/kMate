How it's integrated

cargo_line.py reads the other agent's cargo/results/tfbs_payload/* (read-only — nothing is written back into their tree) and turns it into L_cargo. The line requires four things, not just enrichment:

Enriched against the locus's own dinucleotide-shuffled null (p ≤ 0.05).
Not repeat-driven — I reused the hits-per-motif and tandem measures from the AT4G11800 artefact. The enriched cargo loci run 1.0–3.7 hits per motif against AT4G11800's 11.4, so they pass cleanly; the check currently removes nothing but will catch the next one.
Not led by an artefact family — BBR-BPC is the GA-repeat artefact. (The telomeric 7-mer artefact was already filtered upstream by the cargo agent.)
Landed where a cis-element could act — promoter, UTR, or an ATAC peak. Enrichment in a gene desert is a sequence fact with no route to a phenotype.

24 of 193 insertions are enriched; 6 pass all four. Note that L_cargo can only fire for insertions, so it adds to a candidate but never subtracts, and n_lines isn't comparable between insertions and deletions on its own.

The main finding: the enrichment statistic counts motif hits, not binding sites

The payload counts FIMO hits on the real and shuffled sides alike. That looks fair, but hits inside one element are not independent — at SKS3's insertion a single 44 bp ABRE draws 59 bZIP hits from 23 motifs. A dinucleotide shuffle destroys clustered elements, so it almost never piles hits that way. One real element therefore becomes dozens of counts against a null that can't produce them, and the ratio and the p-value are both inflated.

I re-ran six loci from scratch, merging overlapping hits into distinct sites, everything else identical:

Second finding: 11 of 57 promoter loci name the wrong gene

The dossier title fuses two columns that don't agree: site_cat (from the strand-aware tier) and nearest_gene (nearest by distance). For SKS3's insertion it reads "promoter (<2 kb) of AT5G48460, 73 bp away" — but AT5G48460 is on the minus strand and the insertion is at its 3′ end, a terminator. The gene whose promoter it actually occupies is AT5G48450, 364 bp away.

I checked all 57 promoter-labelled loci against the GFF: 46 agree, 11 name the wrong gene, and in all 11 a correct gene exists. Affected loci include Chr2_18114766, Chr2_13127635, Chr5_19636028, Chr3_9632650, Chr4_2692712. Note nearest_gene is inherited from sv_hit_annotation_loci.csv in our tree, so the root cause is on our side — the cargo dossier just presents it as a promoter claim.

Third: the locus-level null is inconsistent with the family-level one

exp_sites averages only over shuffles that produced ≥1 kept hit (line ~314), while the family-level code divides by the full shuffle count (line 354). For short insertions with many empty shuffles this inflates the expectation — Chr3_11895525's is 5.84 against a true 4.35. It's conservative (understates enrichment) and only bites below ~150 bp, but it means the README's "median enrichment over all 193 is 0.97, so the null is calibrated" rests on a biased statistic.
