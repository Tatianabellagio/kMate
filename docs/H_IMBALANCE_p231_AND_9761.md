# h-imbalance supplementary figure + the "9761 popping" investigation

Session record (2026-06-02/03). Two threads: (1) building the paper supplementary
figure showing how the production k-mer choices correct the founder-vector `h`
imbalance on the p231 panel, and (2) chasing down the one ecotype (**9761**) that
shows up as a spurious outlier in every panel.

## Part 1 — the h-imbalance figure

**Goal.** Supplementary figure: effect of the two production k-mer choices on the
231-founder frequency vector `h`, on the *heterogeneous* p231 panel (80 long-read /
cactus + 151 short-read / PG founders) — the panel where the imbalance exists.

**Deliverables**
- Notebook: `notebooks/H_IMBALANCE_p231.ipynb` — runs in the **`basic`** mamba env
  (has matplotlib; `kmate` does not). **Generated by `notebooks/_build_h_imbalance_nb.py`** —
  edit the builder and re-run it, *not* the `.ipynb` directly (it gets overwritten;
  no `nbconvert` in `basic`, so the builder execs the cells to produce the PNG).
- Figure: `notebooks/plots/h_imbalance/h_imbalance_grid_p231.png` → synced into the
  paper via `paper_svs/sync_figures.sh` (mapping → `Supplemental_figures/`).

**Final grid** — 3 columns × 3 rows, all p231/arch3, Chr1, seed s42, simulation truth:
- Columns: `n231_g0` (uniform, all 231); **n50 long-read-heavy** (40 LR + 10 SR, LR truth
  0.80); **n50 short-read-heavy** (45 SR + 5 LR, LR truth 0.10).
- Rows: **raw** (unfiltered `kmer_pa_p231`, ω≡1) → **private k-mer filter**
  (`kmer_pa_p231_filt2`, ω≡1) → **private k-mer filter + bubble weighting**
  (filt2, ω_k=1/m_b — production).
- Style: greenish (dark green = long-read est, light green = short-read est), grey text,
  no spines, grey gridlines, shared y-axis, RMSE per panel, no figure title, legend at
  bottom; n50 columns ordered by true frequency so selected founders sit right and
  spurious mass on absent founders is visible left.

**Headline result** — long-read `h`-mass (truth → estimate), down the rows:

| column | truth | raw | filter | filter+ω_k=1/m_b |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n231_g0 | 0.35 | 0.56 | 0.47 | **0.33** |
| n50 long-read-heavy | 0.80 | 0.77 | 0.71 | **0.58** |
| n50 short-read-heavy | 0.10 | 0.24 | 0.22 | **0.17** |

raw EM over-credits long-read founders; the filter + weighting walk it toward truth.
**Panel-conditional caveat (M6) is visible:** weighting clearly helps the balanced
`n231_g0` column, but in the *extreme* skewed pools it over-corrects (RMSE/MAE slightly
worse, and it pushes mass onto absent short-read founders) — because ω_k=1/m_b is a
long-read-suppression that only helps when over-credit exists to cancel.

**How the new data arms were produced (reusable)**
- `benchmarks/p231/scripts/07d_run_kmate_raw_p231.sh` — the missing **raw** arm (unfiltered
  matrix densifies to ~21 GB → needs SLURM `--mem`). Reuses each regime's existing reads.
- `benchmarks/p231/scripts/08_build_run_skewed_p231.sh` — the **skewed pools, without VISOR**
  (VISOR lived in the deleted `pang` env). For a g0 pool each founder = one haplotype, so it
  `wgsim`s the per-founder Chr1 haplotypes already in `n231_g0` (`haps/s_ind*/h1.fa`) and pools
  them with the chosen class skew, then runs the 3 arms count-once. gen-0 is clean by
  construction (exactly 50 distinct founders, each truth 1/50 — no with-replacement staircase).
- `07e_run_seedmix_grid_arms.sh` — SEEDMIX (real) arms on the benchmark matrices; computed but
  **dropped from the final figure** per direction (kept on disk under `results/seedmix_grid/`).
- `filt2u` (filter) and `filt2mb` (weighted) arms for the random regimes pre-existed.

**Note on truth.** All columns use the *simulation* truth (`pool_weights.tsv`), not hapFIRE.
`n231_g0` is the fixed-1/231 uniform pool (verified: single distinct truth value, all counts 1).

## Part 2 — the "9761 popping" investigation

**Observation.** One short-read (PG) founder floats high as a spurious outlier in every
panel. It is **always the same ecotype: 9761** — the #1 spurious founder in all 9 panels.
Magnitude: cactheavy raw spurious `h`=0.061 (truth 0); `n231_g0` raw est 0.042 vs truth
0.0043 (~10×). The filter shrinks it, the weighting partly regrows it; it never disappears.

**Hypotheses tested and FALSIFIED** (raw p231 `kmer_pa`, `diag_9761.py`):

| hypothesis | metric | 9761 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defective / low-quality founder | K_f rank 124/231; missingness 17.8% (~median 16.8%); nearest Jaccard 0.66 | unremarkable | ❌ |
| Few private k-mers → non-identifiable | 16,566 private (0.74%), **above** median 4,326; rank 151/231 | more private than most | ❌ |
| Heavily shadowed by present founders | 97.3% of its k-mers covered in cactheavy | **below** absent-SR median 98.2% | ❌ |
| **Most common k-mers** (avg # founders sharing each) | mean_ac 174.5, **rank 78/231** (median 172.5; max 206 = founder 9977) | barely above median | ❌ |
| **Identifiability sink** (NNLS-reconstructable from the other 230) | R²=0.786, **rank 147/231 — below** median 0.793 | not a sink | ❌ |

**What the tests DID explain.** The real NNLS sinks are **7209, 6932, 9643** (R²≈0.94–0.98) —
and 7209/9643 are exactly the **#2/#3 spurious** founders seen in the per-panel scan. So
collinearity explains the *secondary* over-credits. **9761 is the anomaly:** largest spurious
mass, yet structurally bland — not common, not collinear, above-median private k-mers — and
worst in *every* pool (long-read-present, short-read-present, all-present: different founder
sets, same winner).

**Leading remaining hypothesis (UNTESTED).** The over-credit is **count-driven, not geometry-driven.**
The binary presence matrix (all the tests above) collapses repeats to 1, so it is blind to
k-mer *multiplicity*. 9761 likely carries a handful of **high-multiplicity / repeat-region
k-mers** observed at anomalously high counts in *any* A. thaliana read set, dominating its EM
M-step term `Σ_k K[9761,k]·c_k/μ_k`. Pool-independence is the tell. **Test not yet run:**
recount one pool's reads, compute each founder's count-weighted evidence, and check whether
9761's is concentrated in a few very-high-count k-mers (a repeat signature).

**Interpretation for the paper.** Per-founder `h` is non-identifiable / noisy
(BACKGROUND limitation #4); 9761 is the extremal manifestation. The spurious "point mass"
is ≤0.06 and concentrated on a single founder, while the **aggregates** (long/short-read mass)
and the **projected AF** average over it and remain accurate. Best stated as a one-line caveat
in the discussion, not a figure metric (a spurious-mass annotation would also muddy the
weighting story in the skewed columns — see M6).
